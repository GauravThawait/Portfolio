## MAD's first in-house product: a partner CRM

*React · Node.js · PostgreSQL · 2025 – 2026*

MAD tracked partner schools and MOUs in spreadsheets. On my first day, I was asked to replace them on a short timeline. I took an open-source CRM as the base and re-engineered it onto PostgreSQL to fit MAD's relational data. It was used by about **200 staff** for **14 months**, managing a pipeline of **1,000+ leads**, about **1,500 contacts**, and **127 partner-school MOUs** (1–5 year terms) covering **8,000+ children**.

When MAD outgrew it, I ran proof-of-concept deployments of two open-source alternatives, then supported an external contractor's custom build: bringing context from the first version, planning the transition, and building the real-time user sync into the new system.

## E-Office: document management for the Police Department

*Node.js · React · PostgreSQL · AWS · Freelance · Jun 2024 – Jan 2025*

A role-based document management system for the Chhattisgarh Police Department, with audit trails for creating, routing, and tracking official records. Used by **100+ staff across 9 departments** to manage **6,000+ records**, replacing paper-based workflows. It has run in production for over a year.

## Referral attribution for makeadiff.in

*JavaScript · Webflow*

MAD wanted to know which referral brought in each volunteer application and donation. I wrote a JavaScript layer for the Webflow site that captures UTM parameters when a visitor arrives, keeps them across sessions (so they survive the visitor leaving and coming back), assigns a default source to direct traffic, and passes them through to the application and donation pages.
